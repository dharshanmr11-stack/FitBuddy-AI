# Problem Statement

## Project Name
FitBuddy AI

## Problem

Many students find it difficult to create a simple and consistent weekly fitness and wellness routine. They may not know which activities are suitable for their needs, how to organize a 7-day plan, or how to adjust the plan based on their feedback.

## Proposed Problem

There is a need for a simple AI-powered web application that can generate a personalized 7-day wellness and fitness plan based on basic user information and provide safe, general wellness guidance.

## Target Users

- Students
- Beginners interested in fitness and wellness
- Users who need a simple weekly activity plan

## Goal

To develop an easy-to-use AI-powered application that helps users create a simple and personalized 7-day wellness and fitness plan.
